LIVE GAZE

test prints

Every sheet LIVE GAZE prints, filled with a made-up session — two visitors walking between the images in their own order, coming back to some — and made by the same code as the real thing, so a test print here is what a real one will be. Each opens in its own tab, ready to print; nothing is measured, saved or tracked, and every page says TEST PRINT. Made from ….

In the print dialog: pick your printer, keep the scale at default (any scale works for the image sheets, since gaze is mapped through their tags — only don't crop), and set Background graphics on if your browser asks. Save as PDF shows the result without using paper.

type 1 · the image sheet

The image sheet — an image with its four tags

What goes on an easel: one image per page, as big as the sheet allows, its four tags touching it — beside it, so the whole image shows and the tags reach the paper's edge, or on its corners, for a bigger image. A0 is the exhibition's size, for a print shop (save it as PDF); a smaller paper tries your own printer, the colours and the sharpness of the tags. Every image of the session gets its sheet.

One image's analysis — for images on screens

The analysis window of one image as it stands when the image closes: scanpaths, statistics, what the eyes uncovered, and the other images, on one landscape page. Press print image 1 at the bottom left (or p). Dark like the screen it comes from, so it takes a lot of ink.

types 3, 4, 5 · scanpaths, heatmaps, statistics

The end of a session — from the overview

The overview's pop-up as after two visitors finished: the scanpaths on every image, and on request heatmaps (both visitors or one) and fixation statistics — each with its print button, or print all three. On white, in the colour-blind safe orange and blue.

types 3, 4, 5 · on an instax WIDE

The instax cards — the same three, 99 × 62 mm

The overview's prints laid out again for an INSTAX Link WIDE (1260 × 840 dots on instax WIDE film): the images as big as the card allows, numbered, and no text under 2 mm. print on the instax sends a card straight to the printer over Bluetooth through the bridge, with no app or phone: switch the printer on and close the INSTAX app. One film per card, so a test card uses film too.
